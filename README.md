
# 🌙 AURA

### AI-Powered Emotional Wellness & Self-Reflection App

AURA is an Android application that helps users understand their emotions, reflect on their thoughts, and build positive daily habits through AI-powered journaling and mood-based activities.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **AI Emotion Journal** – Get emotional insights from your journal entries.
- 🎭 **Mood Tracking** – Select and record your current mood.
- 🎯 **Daily Challenges** – Receive activities based on your mood.
- 🪞 **Mirror-Tech** – Encourage self-reflection and self-awareness.
- 🧠 **Social Lab** – Explore emotional and social understanding.
- 🌱 **Personal Growth** – Track reflections and progress.
- 🌈 **AURA Avatar** – An interactive digital companion.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Kotlin & Jetpack Compose
- MVVM Architecture
- Room Database & DataStore
- Gemini API
- Hilt Dependency Injection
- CameraX & ML Kit
- Navigation Compose
